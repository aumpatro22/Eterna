# Our Promise to You

**The Eterna Trust Charter**

---

## What This Is

This is not a legal document. This is a personal commitment from the people who build and maintain Eterna to the families who trust us with their most meaningful memories.

Our legal commitments are in our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) and [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). This document goes further. It explains *why* we built Eterna the way we did, and what principles guide our decisions.

*This Trust Charter reflects our current intentions and guiding principles. It is not a legally binding contract. Eterna reserves the right to update this document as our platform evolves.*

---

## You Own Your Memories

Your photographs belong to you. Your stories belong to you. Your voice recordings, your family videos, your handwritten letters, your children's first drawings — all of it belongs to you.

Eterna is the shelf. You are the owner of everything on it.

We do not claim ownership of anything you upload. Your family's private memories are not used to train AI models for third parties without your consent. We do not sell your content.

---

## We Do Not Sell Your Data

Eterna is not an advertising platform. We don't make money by profiling you, tracking your behavior across the internet, or selling your information to data brokers.

Our business model is designed to be simple: we provide a valuable service, and we charge fairly for it. Your attention is not our product. Your data is not our currency.

---

## Your Memories Deserve Dignity

Eterna is designed to be a space free from targeted advertisements and algorithmic manipulation. We believe your grandmother's letters and your child's birthday video deserve a space that respects them.

Some spaces deserve to be protected. Your family's legacy is one of them.

If Eterna ever introduces partnerships or sponsorships in the future, they will be transparent, clearly identified, and designed to respect the dignity of your memories. We will not place ads between your personal content.

---

## Privacy Is a Design Decision, Not a Feature

Privacy is not something we bolted on as an afterthought. It is a foundational principle in how we build.

When we design a feature, the first question is not "How can we get more engagement?" It is "Does this respect the family using it?"

We are intentional about not optimizing for time-on-site. We are intentional about not sending manipulative notifications to pull you back. We are intentional about not using dark patterns to prevent you from leaving.

---

## AI Assists. AI Does Not Own.

Eterna may offer AI-powered tools to help you transcribe recordings, organize timelines, or write tributes. These tools exist to save you time and reduce friction.

But AI is a tool. It is not the author. It is not the owner.

Anything AI creates based on your content belongs to you. If AI transcribes your grandfather's voice recording, that transcription is yours. If AI helps draft a memorial tribute based on your notes, that tribute is yours.

Your personal content is not used to train third-party AI models without your consent. If we introduce opt-in AI features in the future, they will be clearly disclosed with transparent controls.

AI should make preserving memories easier. It should not replace the human meaning behind those memories.

---

## Honest About What We Can and Cannot Do

We believe trust requires honesty, even when the honest answer is uncomfortable.

Here is what we commit to:

- **We will treat your data with care and respect.**
- **We will use reasonable, industry-standard security measures** to protect your information.
- **We will be transparent** about what we do with your data, how we use it, and who has access.
- **We will give you meaningful control** over your content, your sharing preferences, and your account.
- **We will provide tools designed to export your data** so you are not locked in.

Here is what we cannot promise:

- **We cannot promise that Eterna will exist indefinitely.** No company can. If we need to discontinue the service, we commit to providing advance notice and the opportunity to export your data.
- **We cannot claim zero-knowledge encryption** unless we have truly implemented it. We will not use security buzzwords to create a false sense of safety.
- **We cannot promise absolute security.** No platform on the internet can. We will protect your data with diligence and rigor, but we are honest about the limitations of digital security.
- **We cannot guarantee that your data will never be lost.** Hardware fails. Software has bugs. The world is unpredictable. This is why we strongly encourage you to keep your own backups.

---

## Keep Your Own Backups

We say this not to diminish our responsibility, but to increase yours — because these memories matter too much to exist in only one place.

Eterna should be one part of your preservation strategy, not the entirety of it. Keep copies on external drives. Print the photographs that matter most. Store important documents in more than one location.

We will work hard to keep your memories safe. But the memories are yours, and their ultimate safety is in your hands.

---

## Dignity Above Everything

Eterna exists to serve families navigating some of life's most meaningful moments — births, milestones, losses, legacies.

These moments are not engagement opportunities. We are committed to not gamifying grief. We are committed to not exploiting nostalgia for retention metrics.

When someone builds a memorial page for a loved one, that page deserves reverence. When someone records their grandparent's voice before it is lost, that recording deserves protection. When a parent stores their child's first drawing, that drawing deserves a platform that respects it.

Dignity is not a feature. It is our obligation.

---

## If We Get It Wrong

We are human. We will make mistakes. When we do, our intent is to:

- **Acknowledge the mistake honestly.**
- **Communicate clearly** about what happened and what we are doing to fix it.
- **Prioritize your data and your trust** over our pride or convenience.

We would rather admit a flaw and fix it than hide behind corporate language and hope no one notices.

---

## A Final Thought

Eterna was built because we believe that every family's story deserves to be preserved — not on someone else's terms, not surrounded by advertisements, not mined for data — but on your terms, in your space, with your control.

We are grateful that you trust us with something as precious as your family's memories. We do not take that trust lightly.

Thank you for being here.

---

**— The Eterna Team**
