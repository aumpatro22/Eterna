# Privacy Policy

**Eterna — Family Legacy & Memory Preservation Platform**

**Last Updated:** June 24, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Eterna is a private family legacy platform designed to help families preserve memories, stories, photographs, videos, voice recordings, and family history.

Eterna is not a social network. We currently do not operate on advertising, engagement algorithms, or data monetization. We exist to serve families who want to preserve what matters most.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have. We have tried to write this policy in clear, human-readable language. If something is unclear, please contact us at the address listed at the bottom of this document.

By using Eterna, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When you use Eterna, you may voluntarily provide us with the following types of information:

- **Account Information**: Your name, email address, and password when you create an account.
- **Profile Information**: Display name, biography, profile photo, and other details you choose to add.
- **Uploaded Content**: Photographs, videos, audio recordings, voice notes, written stories, family timelines, memorial pages, and other memories you choose to preserve.
- **Communications**: Messages you send through the platform, contact form submissions, and support requests.
- **Family and Sharing Preferences**: Information about who you choose to share content with, including family members or trusted collaborators.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access Eterna, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, and device type.
- **Usage Information**: Pages visited, features used, and general interaction patterns.
- **Log Data**: IP addresses, access times, and referring URLs.
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: Small data files stored on your device to help the platform function properly and remember your preferences.

We collect this information to operate, maintain, and improve the platform. We currently do not use this data to build advertising profiles or sell to third parties.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

- **Consent**: When you create an account, upload content, or opt into specific features.
- **Contract Performance**: To provide the services you have requested and maintain your account.
- **Legitimate Interests**: To improve our platform, ensure security, prevent fraud, and communicate with you about your account. We balance these interests against your privacy rights.
- **Legal Obligations**: When we are required to process data by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing conducted prior to withdrawal.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To provide, operate, and maintain the Eterna platform.
- To create and manage your account.
- To store, organize, and display the memories and content you upload.
- To enable family sharing and collaboration features you choose to use.
- To communicate with you about your account, support requests, and important platform updates.

## 6. Family Sharing and Permissions

Eterna may offer features that allow you to share memories with family members or trusted collaborators. When you share content:

- **You control who can access your content.** Sharing is initiated by you.
- **Shared content may be visible** to the specific individuals you invite.
- **Collaborators may be able to add, edit, or contribute** to shared memorial pages or family spaces, depending on the permissions you grant.
- **Revoking access** will prevent future access, but recipients may have already viewed or saved shared content on their own devices. Eterna cannot control content once it has been viewed or downloaded by others.

We encourage families to discuss and agree upon sharing preferences among themselves.

## 7. AI Features and AI-Generated Content

Eterna may offer AI-assisted features designed to help you organize, transcribe, summarize, or enhance the accessibility of your memories. For example:

- Transcribing voice recordings into text.
- Suggesting organizational structures for timelines.
- Generating written tributes or summaries based on information you provide.
- Improving photo descriptions for accessibility purposes.

**Important disclosures about AI features:**

- AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies, errors, or unintended results. You should review all AI-generated content before relying on it.
- AI features are tools to assist you. They do not replace your personal memories, judgment, or family knowledge.
- AI features do not grant Eterna ownership of your content. Your uploaded materials remain yours.
- Eterna does not currently use your personal content to train general-purpose AI models for third parties without your consent. If this practice changes, we will notify you and provide clear opt-out options.
- AI-generated content should not be treated as professional advice of any kind (legal, medical, historical, or otherwise).

## 8. Children's Privacy

Eterna is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to Eterna without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove that information.

Families may, of course, upload photographs and stories about children of any age as part of their family legacy — this is a core purpose of the platform. However, the account holder must be an adult or meet the applicable minimum age requirement.

## 11. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services.

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account exists and for a reasonable period afterward to fulfill legal obligations or resolve disputes.
- **Uploaded Content**: Retained while your account is active. Upon account deletion, we will remove your content from our active systems within a commercially reasonable timeframe.
- **Backup Copies**: Deleted content may persist temporarily in encrypted backup systems before being permanently removed. We aim to remove backup copies within a reasonable period, though specific timeframes may vary based on technical and operational requirements.
- **Anonymized Data**: We may retain aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you for analytical and product improvement purposes.

**We do not promise permanent or indefinite storage.** Storage depends on the continued operation of the platform, our infrastructure providers, and the terms of your account. We strongly encourage users to maintain their own personal backups of important memories.
